What is Ashtanga Yoga?

Ashtanga Yoga is a structured and energetic style of yoga that combines a fixed sequence of postures with synchronized breathing. It helps improve physical strength, flexibility, endurance and mental concentration.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our Ashtanga Yoga classes are suitable for both beginners and experienced practitioners. Every session is designed to increase stamina, develop discipline and create a strong connection between body, breath and mind.
