What is Labor Management?

Labor Management is a specialized prenatal program designed to prepare expectant mothers for childbirth through breathing exercises, labor positioning, relaxation techniques and mental preparation.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our experienced instructors help mothers build confidence, reduce anxiety and develop practical skills to manage labor naturally and effectively.
