What is Labor Management?

Labor Management is a specialized prenatal program designed to prepare mothers physically and emotionally for childbirth through breathing, relaxation, movement and labor preparation techniques.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our sessions provide guided support to help expecting mothers understand useful labor techniques, manage stress and prepare confidently for childbirth.
