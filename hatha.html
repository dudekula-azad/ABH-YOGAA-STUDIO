What is Hatha Yoga?

Hatha Yoga is one of the oldest and most traditional forms of yoga. It combines physical postures (Asanas), breathing techniques (Pranayama), and mindfulness to create harmony between the body and mind.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our Hatha Yoga sessions are carefully designed for beginners as well as experienced practitioners. Every class helps improve flexibility, posture, strength, breathing, and inner peace.
