What is Garbh Sanskar?

Garbh Sanskar is a traditional Indian practice that combines yoga, meditation, breathing exercises, music and positive thinking to promote the physical, mental and emotional well-being of both mother and baby.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our Garbh Sanskar sessions provide a calm, supportive environment that encourages relaxation, emotional balance and a healthy pregnancy journey.
