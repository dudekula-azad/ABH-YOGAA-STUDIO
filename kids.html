What is Kids Yoga?

Kids Yoga is a playful and engaging practice designed especially for children. Through simple yoga poses, breathing exercises and interactive activities, children develop physical fitness, balance and emotional well-being.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our Kids Yoga classes encourage healthy growth, improve concentration, build confidence and make fitness an enjoyable experience through fun-filled sessions.
