What is Infant Massage?

Infant Massage is a gentle practice that helps improve your baby's physical development, sleep quality and emotional well-being through safe and soothing massage techniques.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our Infant Massage sessions teach parents simple, effective techniques that strengthen bonding while supporting the healthy growth and comfort of their babies.
