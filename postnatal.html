What is Postnatal Yoga?

Postnatal Yoga is specially designed for new mothers after childbirth. It focuses on gentle stretching, breathing exercises and strengthening movements that help the body recover safely.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our Postnatal Yoga sessions support physical recovery, improve posture, strengthen core muscles and promote mental relaxation during the motherhood journey.
