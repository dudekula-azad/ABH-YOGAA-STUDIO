What is Prenatal Yoga?

Prenatal Yoga is specially designed for expectant mothers. It includes safe stretches, breathing exercises and relaxation techniques that support physical and emotional well-being throughout pregnancy.

At ABH Yogaa Studio, our Prenatal Yoga sessions help improve posture, reduce back pain, increase flexibility and prepare mothers for a comfortable and confident childbirth experience.
